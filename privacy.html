Privacy Policy

The short version: Fennel collects nothing, because it sends nothing anywhere.

What Fennel collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign in, no analytics, no advertising identifier, and no crash reporting service.

What stays on your device

  • Every meal you log, including any photo you attach to one.
  • Every symptom and stool entry, with its time and severity.
  • Your evening notes: sleep, stress, cycle if you track one.
  • Your reintroduction program and what you recorded on each challenge day.
  • Your profile answers, and whether you have bought Pro.

All of it is stored in the app container on your iPhone. None of it is transmitted, and the developer cannot see any of it.

This is intimate health information, and it never leaves your phone

What somebody eats, how their gut behaved afterwards and what their stool looked like is about as private as personal data gets. Fennel treats it that way: it is not sent to any server, not shared with any third party, not used to target anything at you, and it appears in no log line, no metric and no notification. There is no version of this app that sends it anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to go.

There is no sync in this version

Fennel 1.0 keeps your diary on the phone it was logged on, and offers no sync of any kind. If sync is ever added, it will go to your own private iCloud account, it will be off until you turn it on, and this page will say so before that version ships.

Photos

A meal photo is a memory aid so you can recognise an entry later. Fennel does no photo analysis and no food recognition, on the device or anywhere else, and this is a permanent design decision rather than a feature that has not been built yet. Photos are stored with the diary entry and are not written to your photo library or uploaded.

Network

The only network activity is Apple handling a purchase or a restore if you choose to buy Pro, and opening a source link if you tap one, which opens in your browser. That is Apple talking to Apple, and your browser talking to a public health service or journal page. Neither one sends anything about your diary.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. Fennel never sees your payment details. Apple provides the app with a receipt confirming the purchase, which stays on your device.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything, permanently, because there is no copy anywhere else. Export first if you want to keep it.

Children

Fennel is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change will appear here and in the app release notes. Any version of Fennel that starts sending data anywhere would be a different app to the one described here.
